# Choice Picker Wheel

## Have you ever been stuck in a situation where a choice needs to be made? 
Well I am always stuck with what to pick as a starter whenever I play a pokemon rom hack.So here's the solution :A picker wheel made with svelteKit that helps you pick the perfect pokemon to start with.

## Here are the perks of this website
- You can enter your choices via textbox on the left side with a dedicated add button
- There is a picker wheel on the right side and a spin button in the middle of the wheel
- When you press the spin button the wheel rotates and a the picker arrow lands on a random choice(of your given choices)
- My website also has clean look with glass morphism implementation
- This website also has a taskbar where you can find the home,contact me and google search section for convinience
